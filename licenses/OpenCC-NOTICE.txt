SubPop statically links the unmodified OpenCC C API and bundles only the upstream
standard s2t/t2s configurations and their required dictionaries. Regional
vocabulary conversion and the Jieba plugin are not enabled. Conversion happens
locally and needs no network or recognition model.

Bundled library dependencies used by OpenCC:
- Marisa 0.3.1, Copyright 2010-2025 Susumu Yata; BSD-2-Clause license option.
- Darts-clone 0.32h, Copyright 2008-2014 Susumu Yata; BSD-2-Clause.

Their notices are reproduced in the accompanying OpenCC-*.txt files.
